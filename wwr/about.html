Rapid Rush Rafting

Rapid Rush Rafting was born from a simple belief: the best way to experience a river is from the middle of it. What started as a few friends sharing their love of whitewater has grown into a team of passionate guides dedicated to giving every guest a day they will talk about for years. Whether you are paddling for the first time or chasing your hundredth rapid, we’re here to make it thrilling, safe, and unforgettable

"History"

We grew up on the water, learning to read currents, find the best lines through the rapids, and respect the power of the river. Along the way we realized that the real magic of rafting isn’t just the adrenaline. It’s the laughter in the boat, the teamwork it takes to get through a tough section, and the quiet moments floating between rapids when the scenery takes your breath away. Rapid Rush Rafting was created to share all of that with others.
