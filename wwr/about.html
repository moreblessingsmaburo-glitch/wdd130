Rapid Rush Rafting

Our mission is to deliver safe, exciting, and unforgettable whitewater experiences for every guest, led by skilled local guides and guided by deep respect for the river. We put safety first, welcome everyone, and keep it fun. Feel the Rush.

History

Rapid Rush Rafting was born from a simple belief: the best way to experience a river is from the middle of it. What started as a few friends sharing their love of whitewater has grown into a team of passionate guides dedicated to giving every guest a day they will talk about for years.

Whether you are paddling for the first time or chasing your hundredth rapid, we’re here to make it thrilling, safe, and unforgettable.
